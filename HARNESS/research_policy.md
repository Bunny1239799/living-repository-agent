# Research Policy

## Evidence Standards
- Separate verified facts, hypotheses, and unknowns.
- Record the source for every material external claim.
- Never fabricate citations, sources, tests, or research results.
- Record the date and relevant context for time-sensitive evidence.
- Identify conflicts and gaps instead of silently resolving them.

## Output
Save research under the active mission's findings directory.
Include the research question, findings, sources, uncertainty, and open questions.

## Limits
Research does not authorize external actions.
A placeholder must be labeled as a placeholder.
