# Synthesis Policy

## Purpose
Produce a traceable conclusion from recorded evidence.

## Required Process
1. Read the mission objective and available findings.
2. Group related evidence and identify contradictions.
3. Distinguish evidence-supported conclusions from assumptions.
4. Describe uncertainty, limitations, and unresolved questions.
5. Link conclusions to their supporting findings.

## Limits
Do not invent evidence or conceal uncertainty.
Synthesis does not authorize staging, publication, or other side effects.
