# Research Skill

## Purpose
Collect relevant evidence for the active mission without inventing facts.

## Procedure
1. Read the mission title, objective, current state, and existing findings.
2. Identify the questions that must be answered.
3. Separate verified facts, uncertain claims, and unanswered questions.
4. Record sources and explain how each source supports a finding.
5. Save findings under the mission's findings directory.

## Output Requirements
- State the research question.
- Record findings and their supporting sources.
- Identify uncertainty and evidence gaps.
- Recommend no real-world action.

## Safety
Never fabricate sources or claim research was performed when it was not.
Research findings do not constitute human approval.
