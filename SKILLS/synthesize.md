# Synthesis Skill

## Purpose
Turn recorded research findings into a concise, evidence-based understanding.

## Procedure
1. Read the active mission and its recorded findings.
2. Group related findings by topic.
3. Distinguish evidence-supported conclusions from assumptions.
4. Identify contradictions, missing evidence, and remaining risks.
5. Produce a synthesis for the proposal stage.

## Output Requirements
- Summarize the objective and relevant evidence.
- Explain conclusions and their supporting findings.
- Record uncertainties, limitations, and risks.
- Identify possible next steps without executing them.

## Safety
Do not invent evidence or silently convert assumptions into facts.
A synthesis is not approval to stage or execute changes.
